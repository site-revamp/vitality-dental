A little more understanding

It is okay to feel nervous about visiting the dentist.

Dental anxiety can make it difficult to arrange the care you need. Vitality Dental welcomes nervous patients and encourages you to talk openly with the team about what would help you feel more comfortable.

  • Discuss your concerns before deciding on treatment.
  • Take time to understand the options available to you.
  • Ask the team about sedation if you feel particularly anxious.
