A DIFFERENT DENTAL EXPERIENCE

It's okay to feel nervous.

We understand that visiting the dentist is not always easy. Previous experiences, uncertainty or anxiety can make taking that first step difficult.

At Vitality, supporting nervous patients is an important part of our approach. We want you to feel listened to and understood.

  • We welcome conversations about your concerns.
  • You can discuss your needs before deciding on treatment.
  • Sedation is available upon request, where clinically appropriate.
  • Our team aims to help you feel more comfortable throughout your visit.
Learn about nervous-patient care ↗

Sedation requires an individual clinical assessment and is not suitable for every patient.
